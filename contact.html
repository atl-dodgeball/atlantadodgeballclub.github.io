Quick answers

Which night should I attend first?+

Tuesday Social Gym is the friendliest starting point if you're new. Sunday is a higher-intensity competitive pickup night.

Do I need to bring a team?+

No. Both weekly sessions are pickup-style, so you can come on your own and join the community.

Where can I see schedule updates?+

Follow @atlantadodgeballclub for weekly posts, cancellations, and club news.
